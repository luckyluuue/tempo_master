Practice polyrhythms by ear and touch

PolyPulse Practice is a rhythm tool for hearing two rhythmic layers at once, such as 3:2 or 4:3 polyrhythms. It is useful for piano practice, percussion, composition, ear training, and any situation where you want to feel independent pulses in both hands.

How to practice 3:2 polyrhythm

In a 3:2 polyrhythm, three evenly spaced notes and two evenly spaced notes occupy the same span of time. Start by tapping only one hand on your thigh, then practice the other hand, and finally combine both hands while listening to the two different sounds.

Built-in metronome

The metronome mode supports quarter notes, eighth notes, triplets, sixteenth notes, and sextuplets. Tempo, meter, sounds, and practice settings are saved in the browser so you can resume quickly.

Debussy Arabesque No.1 practice

Arabesque No.1 mode plays only the right and left hand rhythm positions from a selected bar. It keeps the focus on timing, so you can internalize the triplet layer against the left hand motion.
